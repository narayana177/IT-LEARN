## 📲 How to Install Directly on Your Android Phone

1. **Download the APK**:
   - Right-click or download `IT_Learn.apk` from the `sru/` folder in the project explorer to your phone or computer.
   - Alternatively, transfer `IT_Learn.apk` to your phone via USB, Google Drive, WhatsApp, or email.

2. **Allow Installation from Unknown Sources**:
   - Open your phone's **Files / Downloads** app and tap `IT_Learn.apk`.
   - If prompted: **"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"**:
     - Tap **Settings**.
     - Toggle on **"Allow from this source"**.
     - Press the back button.

3. **Install**:
   - Tap **Install**.
   - Once installation completes, tap **Open**.
